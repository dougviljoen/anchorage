# Product principles

## Geography is the interface

The traveller, anchors, possibilities, routes and memories share one continuous
map. Supporting UI explains the field; it does not turn the field into a
dashboard.

## Possibility, not inventory

Show a small number of composed possibilities. Do not expose the size of the
underlying catalogue unless the traveller explicitly asks to search.

## Explain why now

Every recommendation must make its timing legible. Proximity alone is not a
reason.

## Interrupt with restraint

A notification spends trust. Radar should remain silent unless an opportunity
is personally relevant, unusually convenient, time-sensitive and well
supported.

## Facts have provenance

Hours, dates, routes and availability are evidence. Atmosphere and crowding are
estimates and must be labelled as such.

## Leave room to leave

Every thread has a commitment point, an easy exit, a return path and a fallback.

## Learn without becoming inscrutable

The traveller can inspect and change the taste portrait. “Not today” and “not
for me” are different signals.

## Remember selectively

One photograph, one object and one thought are enough. Anchorage edits memory;
it does not produce a passive surveillance log.
